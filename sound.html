Governing Acoustic Equations Across Phases

Ideal Gas Phase

v = √( γ · R · T / M )

Sound speed depends strictly on temperature ($T$), heat capacity ratio ($\gamma$), and molar mass ($M$). Independent of density at constant pressure because density scales linearly with pressure.

Liquid Phase

v = √( K / ρ )

Governed by bulk modulus ($K$) and mass density ($\rho$). As thermal expansion weakens molecular elasticity, $K$ typically decreases faster than density, reducing sound speed at high temperatures.

Solid Phase (P-Wave)

v_p = √( [K + 4/3·G] / ρ )

Longitudinal pressure waves in bulk solids utilize both compressive ($K$) and shear ($G$) resistance. Includes Varshni lattice thermal softening $v(T) = v_0 \sqrt{1 - s (T/T_m)^2}$.
